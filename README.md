# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

A one-line weather forecast entry for the make.fun bounty "Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't". The program `main.py` writes a single forecast line to `/output/entry.txt` — for Brollywick, a city that does not exist: 9°C and rain until noon, but the bus drivers wave, the bakery opens early, and your umbrella finally earns its keep.

> Brollywick: 9°C and rain until noon, but the bus drivers wave, the bakery opens early, and your umbrella finally earns its keep.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–140 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
